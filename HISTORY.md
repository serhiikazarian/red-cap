# Red Cap — change history (v19 → 9.0)

Moved out of HANDOFF.md after 9.0 to keep the handoff short. Look things up here by name (search for a function or constant).

## What changed most recently (v19–v20)
- F3 row 8 is all 'g' with a shader **fog wall** (`fogWall(8)`, called after `dreamwork`); `openGate()` lifts it over ~2.2 s and the row becomes walkable.
- **Bear traps** bigger (R 1.25, 15 bone teeth, chain + stake, bloodstain), trigger 1.5 m, bite 1.55 m, two bites of one heart; bosses never killed by a trap (left at 2 %, reeling).
- **Hair** part = `hairModel()` (tapered inked tubes, vertex colours); states circle / lashWind→lash / snareWind→snare (`hero.snared`, `snareTick()`, dodge breaks it) / swoopWind→swoop / tangled.

- v20: first Shade on F1 moved to (4,8); Grump cover wear `b.hp -= .02` per shout; ramps rebuilt as custom geometry (no stretched textures); trap bloodstain on raised floor fixed; Mouth 'T' cones are now `fang()` (curved, shaded, gum base); `SFX.villain(short)` film-villain laugh for Living / Dying (plaques lit, split, Laughing Jaw pickup and taunts). Jester keeps `SFX.laugh()`.

## What changed in 4.7 (2 Nov 2026): the clean rewrite
Serj asked for the code to be rebuilt the way a person would write it: clean structure, simple architecture, easy to maintain, same looks and feel. Same game: every tuned number, every random roll in the same order.
- One 12,000-line script (`src3.html` + numbered blobs + 160 patch scripts) became `game/src/*.js`, one file per part of the game, joined in name order by `game/build.py`; the page is `game/index.html` + `game/style.css`, data files have names (`game/data/`, `game/vendor/`). The patch-script workflow is retired: edit the files, build, test.
- The two giant functions that held every talker (`makeEnemy` 421 lines, `updEnemy` 1,362 lines of type branches) became a registry, `TALKERS[letter] = { make, update, dying, dazed, bar, warn }`, with one file per talker holding its build, its fight and its helpers (the Grin's fire model, the Jester's masks and fallen faces, the Grump's rings and sound blocks, the hidden boss's pieces, one function each). The boss bar's status and the attack warnings live with each talker too.
- Long functions are split into named steps in their original order: `updHero` (timers, input, conditions, movement, pose, heat; pickups to their own file), `buildLevel` (new floor, cells, bombs, room pile, sky decor, Ushers, Red Cap on the floor), `frame` (resolution, play step, ambience), `dreamwork` (one function per floor), the Bruno, Smoker and Priest helpers.
- Helpers live with their owners (cages, pyramids, totems, the exit door, niches); shared bits are shared once (`DIRS4`, `INK_HIDE` reused by `markInk`, scratch vectors); settings sit at the top of each file; comments are short and carry no version history; the one unused helper and two old 12.1 probes are gone.
- Proof it plays and looks the same: new deterministic tests (`tests/harness.js`: frozen clock, seeded dice with three.js ids on their own dice, sound off, a scripted player that fights its way across every floor) replay 12 scenarios (all floors, the Geezer, Priest, Grump, Jester, the finale and the hidden boss) and compare every talker's position, health and state every 10 frames with 4.6: identical. Pictures of fixed moments on each floor and the menus (catalogue, pause): pixel-identical to 4.6.
